Before you link platforms

Cross-save is a trust-sensitive feature. The page should tell players to read the current source, confirm account ownership, and avoid switching devices during a long route until the save is verified.

Safe test run

Load a familiar area, change one harmless setting or checkpoint state, then confirm the same state appears on the other platform. Do not test with rare rewards or one-time boss outcomes.

Guide update rule

Every platform guide that mentions saves should include the patch version and last-checked date. This prevents old transfer advice from outranking current official instructions.
