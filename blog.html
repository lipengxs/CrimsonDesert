How to research a Pywel route without turning a preview into a promise

A route note should identify its goal, cite the official material that establishes the setting or feature, and avoid inventing encounter details. Start with the world-map guide, compare it with the official Crimson Desert site and the current Steam listing, then label any unverified step as unconfirmed. A video can show an environment; it is not evidence of a universal boss strategy, drop table, or route requirement.
